In 2024, I dedicated all my energy to the Younger project, which helped me clarify a long-term research goal—AIGAI. This goal certainly includes exploring Evaluation for both AI and GAI. Despite this focus, luck wasn’t on my side, and top conferences didn’t accept the papers related to Younger. But, I remain confident that, one day, these efforts will be rewarded.

For 2025, I hope that my dreams and wishes will come true. I also hope to successfully obtain my PhD and seamlessly transition into my new research career. I continue to remind myself of an ancient proverb: "志当存高远" (Set your aspirations high and aim for great things).
